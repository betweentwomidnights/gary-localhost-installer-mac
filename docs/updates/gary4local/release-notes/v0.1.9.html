gary4local v0.1.9

Stable Audio 3 arrives on the macOS localhost stack for the Gary4JUCE 4.0.0-mac release train.

Highlights

  • Adds local Stable Audio 3 model download, token reuse, and gated-access handling inside gary4local.
  • Adds SA3 generation, loop, transform, and continuation endpoints on the Apple Silicon MLX backend.
  • Adds experimental latent_prefix continuation mode for pingpong-based continuation workflows.
  • Adds SA3 LoRA registration plus prompt-pool building from training sidecars.
  • Adds advanced SA3 output-shaping controls for loudness tuning and experimental DiT precision testing.

Recommended Companion

  • gary4juce v4.0.0-mac
  • Release page: https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce/releases/tag/v4.0.0-mac

Notes

  • This build is distributed outside the Mac App Store as a notarized DMG.
  • Sparkle signatures are included for the in-app updater flow.
  • The recommended loudness path on macOS remains peak normalization plus a gentle limiter; the fp32 DiT toggle stays experimental.
